# Roadmap

The project follows this fixed sequence.

1. Scaffold repository.
2. Detailed prior-art and novelty audit.
3. Prove the selected theorem.
4. Final prior-art/uniqueness check against the theorem actually proved; then freeze the statement.
5. Formalise the frozen theorem in Lean with pinned versions and no `sorry`, `admit`, project-specific axioms, or `native_decide` in the final proof chain.
6. Complete Palomar registration under then-current requirements.
7. Write the research paper around the audited theorem and actual formalisation boundary.
8. Complete arXiv submission under then-current requirements.

This session is limited to stages 1–2 and must end with an audit verdict plus a concrete stage-3 handover.
